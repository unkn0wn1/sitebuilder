# Code of conduct

Skewstone is a small project. Be respectful to the people who use it and the people who work on it.

- Assume good intent, and say what you mean plainly.
- No harassment, insults, or personal attacks. Criticism stays on the work.
- Do not publish someone else's private information.

If something here is broken, open a GitHub issue on [unkn0wn1/sitebuilder](https://github.com/unkn0wn1/sitebuilder) or contact the maintainers the same way you would for any other report on that repository. Maintainers may edit or close comments and issues that break this note.
